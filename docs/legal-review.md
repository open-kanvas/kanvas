# Initial legal documents and acceptance

Public `/legal/privacy` and `/legal/terms`, plus explicit `/legal/2026-10-14/privacy` and `/legal/2026-10-14/terms` routes linked on confirmation. Immutable acceptance version `2026-10-14`. Ordinary screen sign-in uses a deliberate checkbox on email confirmation. Standard connector sign-in instead always shows the linked terms/privacy statement beside Continue with email, followed by explicit email confirmation; the consume endpoint requires acceptedLegalVersion and stores account/version/document SHA-256/time/method inside the same transaction as single-use link consumption/account creation. Both current-device and origin-device approval require it; origin completion is based on that durable approval. Device-code OAuth consent remains a separate code comparison. Standard connector permission confirmation completes from its original browser only. Inspecting a link grants nothing.

Policies describe actual account/session/content storage, authorization, diagnostics, cookies/local storage, sharing, optional push and configured mail delivery; no fabricated corporate identity, jurisdiction, fixed retention guarantee, compliance certification or processor geography. Operator must review those deployment-specific details and the contact before production approval. Account revocation and archival are not promised as content deletion. Legal acceptance records preserve the first acceptance per account/version for version evidence. Future policy releases must retain the old versioned documents/routes and use a new version; never edit a released document in place.

Reference structure was compared with public collaborative-software privacy/terms pages, not copied as substantive commitments; operational statements were grounded in this repository. No claim of legal completeness. Owner legal review is a production follow-up, not a blocker to dev testing.

Public structure references inspected 2026-10-14: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement and https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service (collection, purposes, sharing, security/retention/contact; account, acceptable use, content rights, availability, termination/change sections). GitHub-specific legal bases, entities, geography and guarantees were not adopted. Notion pages were attempted but did not yield usable reference content.
