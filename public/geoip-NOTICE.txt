Optional IP geolocation

Kanvas does not bundle a GeoLite database. Operators may supply their own
GeoLite2-City.mmdb under MaxMind's current GeoLite End User License Agreement:
https://www.maxmind.com/en/geolite/eula

When enabled: This product includes GeoLite Data created by MaxMind,
available from https://www.maxmind.com/.
